How this works

Every key you save here lives in your browser only, under localStorage["op:hub:secrets:v1"]. The Projects / Databases pages read these at page load and include them in the request body when the hub needs to call an upstream that requires auth (the applier sidecar, a Basic-auth SPARQL endpoint, …). Nothing gets written to the hub's SQLite or DuckDB. Clearing your browser data (or hitting Clear all) wipes them.
